# Sharing draft

I made Mini Whiteboard, a small desktop app for quick sketches and screenshot
annotations. Point at one corner and press Ctrl+Alt+W, move to the opposite
corner, then press it again to place a board. You can draw, type, add shapes and
erase without a permanent toolbar.

V1.1 is available for Windows, with a Mac preview for anyone who wants to try it.
The source is open under the MIT license.

[Download it here](https://github.com/nottbradd/MiniWhiteboard/releases/tag/v1.1)
· [Watch the placement demo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nottbradd/MiniWhiteboard/v1.1/docs/media/placement.gif)

If you give it a try, I'd love to hear what works and what needs improving.
